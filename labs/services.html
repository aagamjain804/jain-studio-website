Questions

Before you reach out.

Do I actually own the website once it's built? ▾
Yes, completely. Once final payment is made, the website and everything in it is yours — hosting, code, and data. We don't lock you into a platform or hold anything hostage behind a subscription.
How long does delivery actually take? ▾
Most builds take 3–7 days depending on tier and features. You'll get a live preview link partway through to give feedback before final delivery — not a single big reveal at the end.
What if I need changes after it's delivered? ▾
Every tier includes a bug-fix support window after delivery (7–30 days depending on tier). For ongoing changes beyond that, we offer optional monthly maintenance — entirely your choice, never forced.
Is my data private and secure? ▾
Yes. Your website and its data belong to you alone — we don't share, sell, or use your information for anything beyond building what you asked for.
How do payments work for India vs international clients? ▾
Indian clients pay via UPI or card through Razorpay. International clients pay via Stripe or Wise in USD. Both follow the same structure — 50% advance to start, 50% on delivery.
What if my problem doesn't match any category? ▾
That's most of what we actually build. The categories above are starting points, not limits — describe your problem and we'll tell you honestly whether (and how) we can solve it.
