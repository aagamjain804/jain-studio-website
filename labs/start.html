      Simple agreement.

      Six plain-language clauses. Read once, sign once. No legal jargon.

      1
      Scope of work
      Jain Labs will build a custom website as described in the accepted proposal. Features and scope are fixed once the proposal is signed. Any changes beyond that scope are discussed and quoted separately before work continues.
      2
      Payment terms
      50% of the agreed total is due as an advance before work begins. The remaining 50% is due on delivery. No work starts until the advance is confirmed by Jain Labs. Payment proof must be shared via WhatsApp or email.
      3
      Ownership
      Once final payment is received, the client owns the website and all its contents entirely — code, design, and data. Jain Labs retains no rights, licenses, or access unless separately agreed (e.g. for maintenance).
      4
      Revisions & support
      One structured feedback round is included after the midway preview link is shared. Bug fixes within the agreed support window are included. New features or significant redesigns after delivery are quoted separately.
      5
      Privacy & confidentiality
      All information shared by the client during this project is confidential. Jain Labs will not share, sell, or use client data for any purpose beyond delivering what was agreed in the proposal.
      6
      Cancellation
      If the client cancels after work has begun, the advance payment is non-refundable. Jain Labs may cancel a project and refund in full only if the scope is found technically infeasible after the kickoff call.
